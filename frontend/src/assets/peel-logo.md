# PEEL banana logo

`peel-logo.png` is the transparent version of the approved cartoon banana concept: brown patches, smooth peel interiors, and crisp lowercase lettering on the front flap. The later fibrous-peel and mottled-lettering variation was not used.

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. The source image was the approved concept from the design conversation. Background extraction used `transparent_background: true` and this prompt:

> Background extraction only. Preserve this approved cartoon banana logo exactly: same curved half peeled banana, brown spots, bold dark brown outlines, exact crisp lowercase peel lettering on front flap, same colors and details. Remove the entire plain white background and replace it with actual transparency, preserving cream and pale highlights inside banana. Isolated complete banana with modest transparent padding, no redesign, no shadows, no new elements.

The logo is used in the shared app header and project start page.
